CRYPTOGRAPHIC PRIVACY POLICY

Data Minimization & Confidential Enclave Governance

Last Attested: September 2026 • Compliant with DPDP Act 2025 (Section 8) & EU GDPR Article 25

1. Fundamental Principle: Zero Raw PII Storage

Unlike traditional logging stacks that hoard raw customer prompts, tax identifiers, and credit dossiers in plaintext, Aegis operates on a zero-knowledge mathematical commitment model. Customer identifiers never touch persistent disk storage.

2. CSPRNG Salted Multihashes

Sensitive inputs are bound into cryptographic commitments using a cryptographically secure pseudo-random number generator (CSPRNG) salt. The salt produces a unique Multihash (mh:sha256:...). Once the receipt is sealed, the underlying plaintext is wiped from hardware RAM.

3. Subpoena Selective Disclosure

Under court order or regulatory audit, the institution can selectively disclose a specific field (such as the loan underwriting decision status) along with its specific salt, without revealing the customer's tax ID, debt ratio, or personal identity.

4. Hardware Enclave Isolation (Intel TDX)

Code and data execution occur inside Intel TDX Confidential Virtual Machines. Memory encryption keys are held in hardware CPU registers; cloud providers, host operating systems, and external hypervisors cannot read or alter execution state.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Because plaintext is never stored, there is no customer data to leak in a data breach. Cryptographic receipt hashes are stored in append-only RFC 6962 Merkle trees solely for legal non-repudiation and auditability.
