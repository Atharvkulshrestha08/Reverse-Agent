Slide 1 / 5 The Problem Statement 🎯

The Black-Box Liability Crisis in Autonomous AI

When an AI agent approves a $150,000 loan or denies an insurance payout, there is no receipt. If the AI makes a catastrophic error, organizations have zero legally admissible proof of what actually occurred.

1

What is the Problem?

Enterprises are giving autonomous AI agents financial authority. But standard database logs (Splunk, CloudWatch) are mutable—any database admin can alter rows. Courts reject them as unverified hearsay.

The Privacy Trap: GDPR forbids logging raw customer PII; omitting logs fails audits.
2

Who is Facing It?

14,000+ digital lenders, autonomous InsurTech claims processors, and enterprise SaaS teams facing CFPB, RBI Digital Lending, and European Central Bank audits.

Every company trusting AI with money, health, or legal decisions.
3

Why Urgent Right Now?

EU AI Act Article 12 is active: mandates automated record-keeping for high-risk AI, with fines up to €35M or 7% of worldwide turnover.

84% of enterprise AI deployments are frozen purely due to liability fears.
