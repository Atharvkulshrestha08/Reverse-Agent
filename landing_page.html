Frequently Asked Questions

Everything institutional risk officers, security engineers, and regulators need to know about Aegis.

Aegis packages inferences into canonical RFC 8949 CBOR bytes signed with NIST FIPS 204 ML-DSA-65 and Ed25519 dual keys. Any auditor can verify the post-quantum signature and RFC 6962 Merkle log inclusion mathematically on an air-gapped machine in 6ms without any network dependency or server phone-home.
Customer PII never touches persistent disk storage. Inputs are hashed into cryptographic commitments using CSPRNG salts (mh:sha256:...). Once the receipt is sealed inside the Intel TDX enclave, the plaintext is wiped from memory. Only the applicant or subpoena recipient can reveal the pre-image.
Because the commitment multihash is mathematically bound into the post-quantum signature and the immutable Merkle tree root, altering even 1 bit causes offline verification to fail instantly with an immediate cryptographic tamper alarm (BINDING_HASH_MISMATCH).
Classical RSA and ECDSA signatures will be vulnerable to Shor's algorithm on quantum computers. Because enterprise credit decisions have a 30-year statute of limitations, certificates must resist retrospective decryption and forgery. Combining Ed25519 with NIST FIPS 204 ML-DSA-65 guarantees future-proof permanence.
Integration takes under 15 minutes. Simply wrap your agent inference call with cool.record({ type, metadata, payloads }). The SDK handles enclave attestation, CBOR canonicalization, multihashing, and Merkle tree sequencing automatically.
